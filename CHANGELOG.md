# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-16

- **Breaking:** move general functionality into `touying-ambrosia:0.1.0`; adopt its public API and require Typst 0.15+ / Touying 0.8.0.
- Default fitted agendas to dotted leaders, with theme and per-slide overrides.
- Retain the legacy flavor and cover, with grouped logos, palette-aware backgrounds, and optional team accent strips.
- Organize branding under `assets/logos`; support lab/team marks, including DIAMANT, and adaptive corner logos alongside navigation.
- Provide `greyc-light`, `greyc-ivory`, `greyc-nocturne`, and `greyc-charcoal`; keep team branding independent of palette colors.
- Inherit fitted layouts, navigation, collaborator metadata, appendix numbering, citations, and utilities from the core; expose theme and metadata options for IDE completion.
- Refresh the demo, branding examples, documentation, hero, and starter thumbnail; document artwork permissions and default development installs to `@local`.
